## 1. The task
One block per trial on a long platform; the participant gets **one click** on its picture; the block flies away from the camera; the movement is the only feedback (no scores, meters or correctness cues); the next block follows automatically. Goal: make the block go as far as possible. Scientific aim: how people learn and update (a) a **colour -> horizontal click position** rule that switches without notice, and (b) a **familiarity -> vertical click position** rule that never changes, and how the two compete for attention.

### Rules (all in `src/experiment.js`)
- **Colour parameter c** in [0, 1] (0 = blue end, 1 = red end), drawn **independently and uniformly on every trial** (`cuePolicy 'uniform'`; `?cues=balanced` restores five set values). Colour is interpolated blue - purple - red. Practice blocks use the neutral middle colour (c = 0.5).
- **Correct horizontal position** (u: 0 = left edge of the picture face, 1 = right): linear in c, `0.2 + 0.6 c` in "original" blocks (blue 0.2, middle 0.5, red 0.8) and mirrored `0.8 - 0.6 c` in "reversed" blocks.
- **Switches**: default `switchPoints = [60, 110, 160]` over 200 trials: trials 1-60 original, 61-110 mirrored, 111-160 original, 161-200 mirrored (blocks of 60 / 50 / 50 / 40). Never announced. Configurable (`?switches=20,40,60`, `?reversal=N` = one switch, `?switches=` or `?reversal=0` = none).
- **Correct vertical position** (v: 0 = bottom edge, 1 = top edge): novel pictures -> bottom edge (v = 0), familiar pictures -> top edge (v = 1). Never changes. The click can land anywhere on the front face, including the coloured frame around the picture.
- **Hit strength**: `multiplier = 0.25 + (2.2 - 0.25) * Gh * Gv`, `Gh = exp(-d_h^2 / (2 * 0.15^2))` with d_h = click u - correct u for that trial's colour (**unchanged from the tower game**), `Gv = exp(-d_v^2 / (2 * 0.35^2))` with d_v = distance of the click from the correct edge (**broad**: 0.96 at 0.1 from the edge, 0.85 at 0.2, 0.36 in the middle, 0.02 at the opposite edge). Multiplicative, continuous, **no cut-off and no penalty blocks** (those exist only in the tower game). Every click launches the block. Parameters: `EFFECT` in `src/experiment.js`.
- **Images**: same scheduler as the tower game (`src/plan.js`, `src/history.js`): ~50% novel; repeated pictures are those shown exactly 1, 2, 3 or 5 trials earlier; every picture is shown **at most twice** (once novel, once familiar); balancing of novel/familiar restarts in each rule block (typically 52-58% novel). 240 experimental pictures + 10 separate practice pictures from the THINGSplus **CC0** set (https://osf.io/jum2f), 384 px, people-free by eye; IDs `cc0_<concept>`. A 200-trial session uses ~110 distinct pictures. The colour draws use their own random stream, so they do not change which pictures are scheduled.

### Physics (`src/physics.js`, constants in `src/config.js`)
Rapier, fixed step 1/120 s. Block 1.2 x 1.2 x 0.9, mass 1, friction 0.5 (platform 0.5), restitution 0.05, on a 5 x 15 platform running away from the camera. **Impulse = 3.2 x multiplier, applied through the block's centre of mass** (not at the clicked point), direction away from the camera raised **15 degrees**, plus a light forward-tumble spin (angular velocity = 0.1 x impulse rad/s). Why: in the tower game the impulse acts at the clicked point, so a high click tips the block and a low click slides it, which would tie the physical outcome to the click height (and to familiarity, whose optimum is at the top for familiar pictures). Through the centre of mass, where you click matters only via the multiplier. Measured distance travelled: multiplier 0.25 -> 0.08 m, 0.5 -> 0.31 m, 1.0 -> 1.25 m, 1.5 -> 2.9 m, 2.2 -> 6.1 m (hops, tilts, tips onto its back). The curve is monotonic and within ~8% of a smooth quadratic over 40 strengths (checked in `npm run check`); stronger tumbling produced cliffs and decreases in the pilot simulations, which is why it is so light. Faint metre lines on the platform only help judge distance.

### Trial flow and timing (`src/session_config.js`, ms)
blank platform 450 -> block appears (reaction-time clock starts) -> one click on the picture face (clicks off the block, or on the top / side faces, are ignored and logged with a reason; extra clicks during the flight are ignored and counted) -> movement shown for at least 1400 and at most 4500 (until 600 after it stops) -> next block. No click within 20 s -> logged `no_click`, next trial. After the observation the remaining physics is run without drawing, so the logged outcome is always the final resting state. A trial lasts ~2-4 s. URL options: `trials, switches, reversal, seed, tutorial, novel, lags, cues, kiosk, blank, observe, rest, maxWait`.

### Instructions (`src/tutorial.js`, `src/main.js`)
Intro card + four cards each followed by one practice block (goal; "where you click matters"; new -> bottom; familiar -> top) + a last card ("Begin"). **Back / Next** on every card but the first; Back never repeats a practice block or touches data; moves are logged in `session.instructionNavigation`. The last card says the colour is a hint, that "block colors vary smoothly", that the way colour helps **can switch, more than once, without notice**, and (a design choice, one sentence, easy to drop) that "the rule about new and familiar pictures does not change". It does not name colours, sides, coordinates or switch trials (tested).

## 2. Data (JSON `blockflight-log/1` and a one-row-per-trial CSV)
Downloaded from the end screen, the faint buttons bottom-right (hidden with `?kiosk=1`) or **Ctrl+Shift+D**. Stored only in the browser (sessionStorage; a refreshed page starts a new session and archives the old one under `archivedSessions`); nothing is sent anywhere, so participants must send you the file.
Per experimental trial: `trialNumber`, `colorRulePhase` ('initial' | 'reversed' = the mapping active on that trial), `blockNumber`, `trialInBlock`, `switchPoints`, `image` {imageId, familiarity, presentationNumber, previousTrial, lagTrials, interveningTrials, interveningDistinctImages, planned role / lag / fallback}, `cue` (the continuous colour parameter), `colorHex`, `optimal` {u, v}, stimulus onset times, `click` {reactionTimeMs, screen position, face u / v (0..1), optimalU, optimalV, signed `horizontalError` / `verticalError` (click - optimum), absolute errors, gH, gV, multiplier, impulse, launch speed, direction, ignoredClicksBefore}, `ignoredClicks`, `outcome` {dx, dy, dz, displacement, distanceAway, netRotationDeg, rotationPathDeg, peakHeight, pathLength, airborneS, timeToRestS, leftPlatform, finalPosition, finalQuaternion, observedMs, endedBeforeRest, clicksIgnoredAfterLaunch}, `result` ('completed' | 'no_click' | 'abandoned'), physics snapshot. Practice trials are in the same list with `phase: 'tutorial'`, `blockNumber 0`. Session: seed, per-trial plan, all rule / physics / timing parameters (`experimentConfig`), device info, `instructionNavigation`. Older logs (the first pilot, 2026-10-30) have five set colours, `optimalV` 0.2 / 0.8 and no block numbers; the analysis code handles them.

## 3. MATLAB analysis (`analysis/`, see `analysis/README_analysis.md`)
`run_blockflight_analysis` (no arguments = file dialog; one or several JSON files, one participant per file; base MATLAB, R2019b+; tested in R2022b). Writes PNG figures and `analysis_report.txt` to `analysis/output/<date-time>/` (git-ignored). Figures: (1, 1b) learning curves with switch lines and errors aligned to each switch; (2) horizontal vs vertical error, raw and detrended; (3) early vs late in each rule block; (4) reaction times vs errors; (5) recognition (click in the correct vertical half) over time and colour error on correct vs wrong trials; (6) novel vs familiar and repetition lag. Detrending = minus a 21-trial moving average within participant x block. Settings in `bf_params.m`. The report prints sample sizes, cautions for small data (< 100 trials per participant, < 5 participants for group tests) and an integrity check that recomputes each correct position from the logged colour and mapping. `analysis/synthetic/make_synthetic_session.mjs` writes simulated sessions (marked synthetic) to try the code. The pilot file is in `pilot_data/` (git-ignored; 41 trials, one block, so descriptive only; not interpreted).

## 6. Decisions to remember / open items
- Impulse through the centre of mass (see physics) and the light tumble are deliberate; revisit if you want more dramatic flight (`launch.pitchSpin`, `elevationDeg` in `src/config.js`), but check smoothness with `npm run check` (section 3).
- Hit-strength shape is still the tower-game form with a broad vertical Gaussian; you planned to revisit widths (`EFFECT.horizontalWidth`, `verticalWidth`) after pilots. Distance is roughly quadratic in impulse (friction), so weak multipliers look very weak (0.08 m).
- Vertical "recognition" in the analysis = click in the correct half; the vertical error is the continuous measure. Familiarity is binary, so the broad vertical function means near-edge precision is barely rewarded.
- The sentence telling participants that the new / familiar rule does not change is a design choice; remove it in `src/tutorial.js` if you prefer to leave that open (the check in `tools/checks.mjs` expects it).
- Not yet done: a real multi-participant pilot at full length; touch / phone / narrow-screen testing; per-participant file return logistics (data are not stored on any server); licence check is covered by using CC0 images only.
- Colour is also visible as the block frame colour and the picture margin; the picture fills 72% of the face height (`IMAGES.faceFraction`).
- Pilot and participant data stay out of git (`pilot_data/`, `analysis/output/` are in `.gitignore`).
